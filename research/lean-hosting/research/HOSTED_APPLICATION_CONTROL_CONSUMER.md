recipe, archive provenance, existing eight substitutions, and six earlier
consumer policies remain unchanged.

`:result` key or an existing value. Consuming the returned owned `Except` must
preserve that entry slot's presence and value before the next publication
boundary. Failure keeps the actual failed checkpoint. This change restores
only that register; it does not hide unrelated control changes.
